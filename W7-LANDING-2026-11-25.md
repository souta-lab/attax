## What W7 is (source of truth)

Configuration "W7" from lane `attax-hoiko-night`, final report lanechat
#8403, full record in `bench-results/night-2026-11-24/README.md`
(committed with this landing). W7 was an unlanded working-tree
configuration: its tables lived only as
`attax-hoiko-night/tables-w7-surface.bin` / the night bench-results copy,
and ROWS=40 existed only as a run-time env setting. This branch lands it.

W7 = two kept changes vs the previously shipped configuration:

1. **ROWS=40** — the TBP adapter shows the engine the full 40-row board.
   With the old 20-row view the engine played blind whenever its stack
   grew past row 19 (a structural defect, not a tuning choice).
   Landed as: `tbp/attax-tbp.ts` now defaults `FIELD_ROWS` to 40;
   `ATTAX_TBP_ROWS=20` restores the old view.
2. **Surface tables** — `gpu/tables.bin` is now the W7 file,
   sha256 `783225aba27efffb524d812fedc79655a44353e91525981d07e9b24d69dcc39e`,
   and `PINNED_TABLES_SHA256` in `tools/s2-lab/gpu-player.ts` is updated
   to match (plus the sha mentions in `gpu/README.md` / `gpu/DESIGN.md`).
   Deltas vs config O (index: old -> new), per the night README:
   150 bump -8->-14; 151 bumpSq -3->-6; 152 rowTransitions -6->-10;
   155 tslot 60->150; 156 single -60->-160; 157 double -40->-100;
   158 triple -30->-50; 159 tetris 60->120; 160 mini -20->-60;
   161 tspin1 40->80; 162 tspin2 120->200; 163 tspin3 160->260.
   (tabf165 attack weight unchanged at 90.)
   The landed file was verified byte-identical (sha256) to the night
   lane's `tables-w7-surface.bin`, and its 180-double payload was
   re-read at landing time: indices 150/151/152/155–163/165 hold exactly
   the values above.
